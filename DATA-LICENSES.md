# Data in this site

This site is the web viewer of https://github.com/AriyaLuesawat/phuket-traffic-flood-sim (non-commercial research).
The files under `data-c/` are derived from:

- OpenStreetMap roads, buildings, places, land and water: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The derived data stays under ODbL.
- FABDEM V1-2 elevation (`data-c/dem/`, `grid`): Hawker et al. (2022), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://data.bris.ac.uk/data/dataset/s5hqmjcdj8yo2ibzi9b4ew3sn). Non-commercial use only; the derived tiles are shared under the same licence.
- ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200: CC BY 4.0 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221).
- ERA5 rainfall via Open-Meteo: CC BY 4.0, contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information.
- Simulation results (`data-c/runs/`): produced by the project with SUMO.

Loaded live from other services, not stored here: EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Open-Meteo current weather (CC BY 4.0), ThaiWater rain gauges.
